Privacy policy

CIRCArt, the art timeline game · Last updated: [DATE] · Deutsch

CIRCArt is a free hobby project. I collect as little as possible: enough to save your games, run games with friends, show leaderboards and fix bugs. There are no ads, I don't sell data, and there's no tracking across websites.

2. Playing without an account

You can play solo without signing in. Your game then stays on your device. The app saves settings (language, light/dark theme, whether you've seen the introduction, recently shown cards, unsent games) in your browser's local storage. This is needed for the features you use, so no consent is required (§ 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021). Nothing in it is sent to me unless you sign in. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

3. What is stored when you play online

Guest account

When you join a game with friends or play the daily challenge, the app creates a guest account: a random ID, with no name or email needed. A session token in your browser keeps you signed in. You choose a display name that other players in your games see.

Sign-in with email or Google

If you sign in with email, I store your email address and send you sign-in links. If you sign in with Google, Google tells me your name and email address; I use only the first name as a suggested display name. You can pick a username for the leaderboards.

Games

Your solo games (score, cards, right or wrong, bonus answers), your online games, answers, timings and points. Your username (never your email) appears on public leaderboards. Guests and players without a username don't appear there.

Legal basis for all of section 3: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. This data is needed to provide the game you asked for.

4. Feedback

If you send feedback, I store the topic, your message, the card on screen, the round, the page address (without parameters) and your browser type (user agent), so that I can reproduce bugs. If you're signed in, it's linked to your account. Please don't put personal details in the message.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in fixing problems.
Retention: browser type and page are removed after 90 days. The message is deleted 12 months after I've dealt with it, and after 24 months at the latest.

5. Notifications

Only if you switch them on (and allow them in your browser) do I store your device's push address, its encryption keys and the browser type, so I can tell you when it's your turn. The messages travel through your browser maker's push service (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple). They are end-to-end encrypted, so that service can't read them.
Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Switch it off any time in the profile or in your browser settings.
Retention: until you switch it off, the device is gone, or after 6 months without playing. Sent messages are deleted after 14 days.

6. Visitor statistics

I count visits and a few events (for example "game started") with GoatCounter (operated from Ireland; servers in Germany and Finland). GoatCounter stores no IP addresses, no full browser string and no identifiers, and puts nothing on your device. I only see totals (pages, countries, browser types).
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in knowing whether the game is used. Blocking the script with any content blocker is fine; the game works the same.

8. How long I keep data

Account and game data: until you delete your account. Guest accounts that haven't been used for 6 months are deleted automatically, with all their games. For feedback and notifications, see sections 4 and 5. Deleted data can stay in the provider's encrypted backups for up to [7] days.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15), to rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), and to withdraw consent at any time, for the future (Art. 7(3)).

  • Delete your account: Profile → "Delete account". Your profile, games, answers and devices are deleted immediately. Feedback you sent stays, but no longer linked to you and without browser details. Write to me if you want it gone too.
  • Download your data: Profile → "Download my data" [once available], or email me.
  • Change your name or username in your profile.

I answer within one month. You can also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at) or to the authority in your own EU country.

10. Children

CIRCArt is meant for players aged 14 and over. In Austria you can consent to online services yourself from 14 (§ 4(4) Austrian Data Protection Act). If you're younger, please ask a parent before creating an account or switching on notifications.

11. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The database only answers through checked functions: players can see only their own data and what the game shows to everyone. Access to the admin accounts is protected by two-factor authentication.

12. No automated decisions

There's no profiling and no automated decision-making that affects you legally or similarly (Art. 22). Scores and rankings are just the game.
