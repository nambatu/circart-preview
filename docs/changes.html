PREVIEW · Fri 20 Nov, 22:05

What's new

  • Live games with friends now keep the painting on screen while you wait and unlock its story once your answer is locked in.
  • While others are still thinking you can bet +25 points with Call-It or send quick emoji reactions, followed by a player-by-player breakdown on reveal.
  • Add friends on Profile by username or invite link to view their badges and stats and switch every Ranks board between Everyone and Friends.
  • Games over a few days now let you invite friends directly to their Home screen without sharing a lobby code, alongside Price mode and Painting of the Week.

What to test

  1. Add a friend on Profile or open your friend link in a second browser, check their profile sheet, and toggle Friends on Ranks.
  2. Start a game with friends to try direct Home invitations or lock in early in a live round to test Call-It and the round breakdown.

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your live game is not affected.
