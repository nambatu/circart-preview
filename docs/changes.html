What's new

  • Solo game in German too: all buttons, messages and the end screen (the artwork texts stay English for now)
  • The PREVIEW banner no longer covers the score during a game
  • NEW: Monthly Run, as many tries as you like, 3 lives, your best run counts on the monthly board (November theme: Masterpieces Marathon)
  • Solo game: tap answers instead of typing ("Who made it?" / "Which movement?", +1 each), with the green/red flash
  • German: the app follows your phone language (Profile → Language to switch)
  • Profile → "Send me a test notification" (test version only)
  • The daily no longer uses the monthly theme; the monthly board now shows best runs
  • Test version only: replay the daily challenge as often as you like ("Play again" on the results screen)
  • Live games: everyone's points now stay hidden until the reveal (no peeking at who got it right)

What to test

  1. Play a Monthly Run, then "Again", and check Ranks → Month
  2. Solo game: answer the tap questions before placing the card
  3. Profile → Language: switch between Deutsch and English
  4. Turn on notifications (Profile), then "Send me a test notification"
  5. Play the daily: watch the green/red flash; answer both bonus questions
  6. Create a game, switch the bonus questions off in the lobby, play a round
  7. Async game: after your turn, tap "Turn on" for notifications (iPhone: add to home screen first). Then have a friend finish their turn
  8. Profile → Turn notifications on/off
  9. Finish a live game with a second device, tap Play again on one, join from the other

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.
