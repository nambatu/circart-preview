PREVIEW · Thu 5 Nov, 16:45

What's new

  • Test version only: replay the daily challenge as often as you like ("Play again" on the results screen)
  • Live games: everyone's points now stay hidden until the reveal (no peeking at who got it right)
  • The name you type for one game no longer replaces your profile name
  • After an update the app reloads itself (no more half-old, half-new screens)
  • Clear feedback after each card: a green ✓ or red ✗ flash, and a short buzz on Android
  • Second bonus question: "Which movement?" (+100), next to "Who made it?" (+200); your answers turn green/red
  • Host switches in the lobby: Artist bonus and Movement bonus on/off (both off = the raw game)
  • Notifications for async games: "your turn", a reminder 3 h before a turn closes, and "game over" (turn on after your first async turn, or in Profile)
  • Fixed: the artist was visible in your timeline while the bonus question was still open
  • Play again: after a game with friends, one tap starts a rematch, and the others see "X wants a rematch"
  • Share your result with coloured squares (🟩 right, 🟥 wrong, 🎨 artist named too)

What to test

  1. Play the daily: watch the green/red flash; answer both bonus questions
  2. Create a game, switch the bonus questions off in the lobby, play a round
  3. Async game: after your turn, tap "Turn on" for notifications (iPhone: add to home screen first). Then have a friend finish their turn
  4. Profile → Turn notifications on/off
  5. Finish a live game with a second device, tap Play again on one, join from the other
  6. Share a result and paste it into a chat
  7. Play today's daily challenge from the home screen, then open Ranks
  8. Solo game on a laptop: after placing, can you read the trivia and press Continue?
  9. Pick a username in Profile (after signing in by email) and check you appear on the boards
  10. Open the preview, play a solo game, then check Ranks

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.
