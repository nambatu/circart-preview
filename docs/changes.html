PREVIEW · Thu 26 Nov, 17:00

What's new

  • iPads now also get the 'add CIRCArt to your home screen' hint for notifications (they pretend to be a Mac, so they were missed before).
  • If CIRCArt is opened inside Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok and similar apps, the notification ask now explains that notifications only work after opening it in Chrome or Safari.
  • After your first finished daily, CIRCArt asks once if it may send you one daily reminder, and you pick the time (7, 9, 13, 18 or 20 o'clock). It only comes if you haven't played the daily yet. 'Not now' asks again in a week, 'Never ask me again' stops it.

What to test

  1. Open the preview link from a WhatsApp or Instagram message, play the daily, and look at the reminder pop-up.
  2. On an iPad in Safari: finish the daily and check the home screen hint.

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your live game is not affected.
