PREVIEW · Fri 6 Nov, 16:15

What's new

  • Monthly Run: every card you place right is 1 point, with no artist or style questions. Runs already played were recounted the same way
  • Games with friends: with the timer on, pick the seconds per card (10 to 90)
  • Packs are strictly separate now: their cards only appear when you choose "Play this pack" in the Gallery
  • Story dailies: a daily challenge can now tell a story. You see an intro before the first card, a new part after each card and an ending on the results (the first story comes next)
  • Games with friends: new "Timer" switch in the lobby. It is on by default for live games and off for games over a few days
  • Artist and style questions: you see the right answer in green and your wrong pick in red again, even when the bonus time ran out
  • One tap per question. After your first pick the other names are locked
  • The painting stays visible next to the questions (tap it to zoom). With the timer on, you also see the bonus countdown

What to test

  1. Play a Monthly Run: your score is the number of cards placed right
  2. Create a game with friends, switch the timer on and pick 45 seconds
  3. Play a normal solo game: only your 230 base cards

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your real game at circart.langschwerts.de is not affected.
