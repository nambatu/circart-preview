PREVIEW · Thu 19 Nov, 18:30

What's new

  • When you sign in to an account you already have on a device where you played as a guest, your games from that device now come along: solo games, card packs and games with friends. The daily and the Monthly Run stay behind, so nobody gets a second try.
  • Sign-in fixed: a device that played as a guest can now sign in to an existing account. Email sign-in sends a 6-digit code that you type into the app.

What to test

  1. On a device where you never signed in: play a solo game as a guest, then Profile → Use email instead → your Gmail address. After the code, the message should say your games came along.
  2. Check the Ranks tab: your username is back and your games are in your stats.

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your real game at circart.langschwerts.de is not affected.
