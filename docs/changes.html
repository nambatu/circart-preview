PREVIEW · Fri 20 Nov, 18:55

What's new

  • Four new badges on your Profile: Daily Brushstroke (7/30/100 days in a row), Masterpiece (all cards right in a daily), Signature Spotter (100 artists named) and Photo Finish (won a game with friends by under 100 points).
  • Badges are worked out by the server from your games, so nobody can give themselves one.

What to test

  1. Open Profile after some played games and look for the new badges next to the monthly medals (they appear only once earned).
  2. Check the badge names and lines in German too.

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your live game is not affected.
