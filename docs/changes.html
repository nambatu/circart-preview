PREVIEW · Fri 13 Nov, 00:30

What's new

  • Security: after 8 wrong game codes you have to wait ten minutes, so nobody can guess their way into a lobby.
  • Security: limits on how many lobbies and solo games one player can create, and notifications only go to the real push services.
  • Privacy: the visitor counter (GoatCounter) is gone. The privacy page says so, and deleting your account now also switches off notifications in the browser.

What to test

  1. Join a game with a friend's code: it still works as before.
  2. Type a wrong code: you get "No game with that code."
  3. Switch notifications off and on again in your profile, then use "Test notification".

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your real game at circart.langschwerts.de is not affected.
