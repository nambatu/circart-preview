PREVIEW · Fri 20 Nov, 21:30

What's new

  • After placing a card you see no right or wrong until you lock in artist and style, and tapping answers no longer redraws the screen.
  • After lock-in one headline sums up the card (Picture perfect, Almost a masterpiece, A good first sketch, Back to the drawing board) and a year box says if the spot was right.
  • If the spot was wrong, the year box shows where you put the card and where it belongs, for example "just one card off".

What to test

  1. Place a card, then tap different artists and styles: nothing flashes and nothing jumps.
  2. Place a card one spot too early or too late and look at the year box after locking in.
  3. Get the year right but one bonus wrong and check the amber headline.

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your live game is not affected.
