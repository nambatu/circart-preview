PREVIEW · Fri 20 Nov, 01:44

What's new

  • Solo play and Card Packs now run on the same game screen and round flow as Daily and Games with Friends.
  • For each card you place the artwork on the timeline first, then lock in your artist and style choices before seeing the year and story.
  • Wrong cards still stay off your timeline in Solo, and your score counts 1 point per right answer with coloured squares and your full timeline at the end.

What to test

  1. Start a 5-card Solo game and place one card wrong to make sure it stays off your timeline, then change your mind on an artist or style before locking in.
  2. Open an unlocked pack in Gallery and play a round to check that it uses the same game screen cleanly in both light and dark mode.

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your live game is not affected.
