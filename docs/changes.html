PREVIEW · Thu 5 Nov, 23:00

What's new

  • Safety: feedback and player names are checked for hidden instructions (prompt injection) and invisible characters
  • NEW: card packs. "Baroque Masters" (finish 3 Monthly Runs) and "German Artists" (play the daily 3 days in a row), 25 extra cards each, earned by playing. Unlocked packs mix into your solo games, or play them on their own (home screen → Card packs)
  • Privacy: Profile → "Download my data" and a privacy page
  • The packs panel sits at the bottom of the home screen
  • "How to play": a short tutorial for new players (4 steps, shown once; Profile → How to play opens it again)
  • Monthly Run winners: from 1 December, Ranks → Month shows last month's top 3 (🥇🥈🥉), and winners get a badge in their Profile
  • Solo game in German too: all buttons, messages and the end screen (the artwork texts stay English for now)
  • The PREVIEW banner no longer covers the score during a game
  • NEW: Monthly Run, as many tries as you like, 3 lives, your best run counts on the monthly board (November theme: Masterpieces Marathon)

What to test

  1. Home → Card packs: check the progress, unlock one (3 Monthly Runs) and tap "Play this pack"
  2. Profile → Download my data
  3. Play a Monthly Run, then "Again", and check Ranks → Month
  4. Solo game: answer the tap questions before placing the card
  5. Profile → Language: switch between Deutsch and English
  6. Turn on notifications (Profile), then "Send me a test notification"
  7. Play the daily: watch the green/red flash; answer both bonus questions
  8. Create a game, switch the bonus questions off in the lobby, play a round

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your real game at circart.langschwerts.de is not affected.
