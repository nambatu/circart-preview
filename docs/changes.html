PREVIEW · Thu 5 Nov, 17:00

What's new

  • Play again: after a game with friends, one tap starts a rematch, and the others see "X wants a rematch"
  • Share your result with coloured squares (🟩 right, 🟥 wrong, 🎨 artist named too)
  • Leaving a running live game asks first
  • Daily challenge card now sits at the top of the home screen
  • Daily challenge on the home screen: 10 cards, same for everyone, one try
  • Leaderboards in Ranks: today, this month, all time, plus your online and solo stats
  • Solo game on laptops: result, trivia and Continue no longer cut off
  • New home screen with tabs: Play, Ranks, Profile
  • Dark mode (Profile → Theme)
  • Guest accounts start automatically; sign in with Google or email to keep your stats
  • Solo games are saved to your account (Ranks shows your stats)
  • Online games with friends: live (20 s per card, faster = more points) or async (24 h per turn)
  • After placing a card, pick the artist out of 4 for +200
  • Feedback is saved in the database (Profile → Send feedback, or the flag in a game)

What to test

  1. Finish a live game with a second device, tap Play again on one, join from the other
  2. Share a result and paste it into a chat
  3. Play today's daily challenge from the home screen, then open Ranks
  4. Solo game on a laptop: after placing, can you read the trivia and press Continue?
  5. Pick a username in Profile (after signing in by email) and check you appear on the boards
  6. Open the preview, play a solo game, then check Ranks
  7. Switch to dark mode and look around every screen
  8. Play → Friends → create a live game, open the invite link in a private tab, play it through
  9. Create an async game and take your turn; the other player has 24 h
  10. Sign in with email (link arrives by mail) and check your name and stats stay
  11. Send feedback about anything that feels off

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your real game at circart.langschwerts.de is not affected.
