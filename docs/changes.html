PREVIEW · Thu 19 Nov, 17:30

What's new

  • Sign-in fixed: if you played as a guest on a device, you can now sign in to an account you already have. Before, this was refused, which locked people out on a second device.
  • Email sign-in now sends a 6-digit code that you type into the app. It works on any device, also when you read the email on your phone and play on your laptop.

What to test

  1. On your laptop: Profile → Use email instead → your Gmail address → Send code, then type the code from the email.
  2. Play a round as a guest on another device, then sign in there with the same email: you should land in your account with your username.
  3. Type a wrong code: you get a clear message and can ask for a new one.

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your real game at circart.langschwerts.de is not affected.
