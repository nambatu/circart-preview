PREVIEW · Tue 17 Nov, 17:00

What's new

  • The Gallery is now a collection: paintings you have played show in full colour, the ones you haven't met yet are grey and blurred without title or year, and a counter says how many you have discovered. Paintings you placed right at least once get a small gold star.

What to test

  1. Open the Gallery: most cards should be grey, with a counter like '12 of 230 discovered' on top.
  2. Play a solo card, then look again: that card should now be in colour, with a gold star if you placed it right.

Something wrong? In the app: Profile → Send feedback, or the flag during a game. It lands in the preview database and the next evening build reads it.

This is a test version with its own database. Your real game at circart.langschwerts.de is not affected.
